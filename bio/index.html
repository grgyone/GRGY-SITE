Bio

GRGY (Grgy Sukhanov) is a contemporary artist based in Saint Petersburg, Russia, working across digital art, drawing, street art, and physical objects.

His artistic practice grew out of an interest in street culture, graffiti, posters, and animation. Around 2010, he created Kloud, a character that gradually evolved from a drawing into a recognizable visual symbol.

Today, GRGY develops the Composition and Reworked series alongside street works, sculptures, accessories, and objects, experimenting with different materials and formats.

In 2025, he presented COMPOSITIONS: Selected Works 2022–2024, a solo digital exhibition on OnCyber. His work has also appeared in Visual Art Journal.
