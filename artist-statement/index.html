Artist Statement

I create my own visual world, one that has long revolved around Kloud — a character that began around 2010 as a simple drawing of a black cloud. Over time, it changed into a monster, a sheep-like figure, an abstract form, and eventually became a symbol and my personal signature.

I'm drawn to the process of making things and the freedom to rework whatever catches my attention. In a way, GRGY is a parasite — a monster that absorbs the imagery it likes and transforms it into something of its own. I don't limit myself to one medium. I work with digital images, drawings, canvases, public spaces, sculptures, accessories, and everyday objects. These are different ways for the same visual world to exist.

In Composition, I work spontaneously with shapes and a limited palette. In Reworked, I bring the GRGY aesthetic into found imagery. The street is an open exhibition space for me, a way to make my work visible beyond the internet.

Now I'm interested in moving beyond the character itself. I want the GRGY world to remain recognizable even without Kloud's direct presence — through its forms, combinations, atmosphere, and visual language.
