COMPOSITIONS

Selected Works 2022–2024

Virtual Exhibition, June 2025

GRGY (Grgy Sukhanov)

Format
Independent Virtual Exhibition
Platform
ONCYBER

COMPOSITIONS brings together selected works from 2022–2024 in an independent virtual exhibition by GRGY (Grgy Sukhanov), opened on ONCYBER in June 2025. Rooted in the experience of love and personal transformation, the works explore the tension between creation and destruction through cloud-like figures, inverted hearts and yellow beams.

ENTER EXHIBITION ↗

The space and Kloud

The virtual space places framed works around a gallery with exposed timber beams and overhead light. A three-dimensional Kloud character occupies the centre of the room, connecting GRGY’s graphic language with the spatial experience of the exhibition.
