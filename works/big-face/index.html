In 2023, I placed a large-scale Kloud in an architectural opening of the former Imperial Railway Station in Tsarskoye Selo. The character appears to be looking out of the building, with a tear running from his eye.

The work remained visible for about a week. Shortly after its appearance, the building was completely fenced off for restoration.

Kloud became part of this space for only a brief moment. The project survives through photographs and digital documentation.

Historical context

The Imperial Railway Station was built in 1911–1912 to a design by Vladimir Pokrovsky. It was intended to serve the imperial family’s railway journeys.
