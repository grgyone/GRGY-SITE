NFT. Stage of Acceptance

Year
2022
Project Type
Group Exhibition / Painting
Medium
Digital originals and painted canvases
Location
Here on Taganka Gallery, Moscow, Russia

Two related works, Fall in Love and Heartbreak, began as digital images and were later translated into paintings on canvas for the 2022 group exhibition NFT. Stage of Acceptance at Here on Taganka Gallery in Moscow. Both images use variations of Kloud, surrounded by red hearts, to stage opposing emotional states.

The presentation at the exhibition included censorship, as described by the artist. The digital originals and photographs of the physical works are shown together here, making the differences between the versions visible. The small word-bearing hearts visible in the digital images are absent from the canvases shown in the documentation; the exact circumstances of these changes are not documented here.

The project records a movement from the digital image to the physical exhibition space, with photographs of the painting process, completed canvases and gallery installation.
