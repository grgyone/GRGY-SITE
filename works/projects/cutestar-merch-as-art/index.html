Cutestar / Merch as Art

Project Type
Artist project and exhibition
Medium
Drawing and embroidered T-shirt
Location
MEGA Dybenko, Saint Petersburg
Exhibition dates
15–30 April
Invitation
Denis Fabrika / Dvor 759

Cutestar was an early GRGY project to create an artist-designed T-shirt using his visual language. The T-shirt was his first experience of creating embroidered merchandise.

The T-shirt was later presented at the physical exhibition Merch as Art at MEGA Dybenko, Saint Petersburg. The supplied event text gives the dates as 15–30 April and the venue as the ground-floor atrium.

GRGY participated at the invitation of Denis Fabrika and the Dvor 759 project.
