MUSIC LOVERS WORLDWIDE

2022 · Digital Animation / Public Art

GRGY (Grgy Sukhanov) contributed an original animation to MUSIC LOVERS WORLDWIDE, a collective campaign by STUDIO 21 × NFTBastards × +111°, shown on Moscow media façades. Artists from NFTBastards and the creative association +111°, together with hip-hop radio station STUDIO 21, invite you on a space journey to new galaxies. Together with White Punk and Malenkiy Yarche, you visit planets to record a unique track.

Memory #27

2022 · Collaborative Digital Artwork

Memory #27 is a collaborative digital artwork created by Tim Baym and GRGY in 2022. The project places Kloud within a dark three-dimensional scene featuring a burning car, smoke, and a human figure. GRGY's recognizable graphic language contrasts with the realistic environment, bringing a cartoon-like character into an unexpected setting. Combining 2D graphics and 3D animation, Memory #27 was featured in a December 2022 interview with Tim Baym published by Sobaka.ru.
