MUSIC LOVERS WORLDWIDE

Year
2022
Project Type
Digital Animation / Public Art
Campaign
STUDIO 21 × NFTBastards × +111°

GRGY (Grgy Sukhanov) contributed an original animation to MUSIC LOVERS WORLDWIDE, a collective campaign by STUDIO 21 × NFTBastards × +111°, shown on Moscow media façades.

Artists from NFTBastards and the creative association +111°, together with hip-hop radio station STUDIO 21, invite you on a space journey to new galaxies. Together with White Punk and Malenkiy Yarche, you visit planets to record a unique track.

Documentation: Moscow media façades
