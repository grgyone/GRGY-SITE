ChikoRoko × GRGY — Kloud

Year
2022
Project Type
Collaboration
Medium
Digital collectible toy
Collaborators
ChikoRoko × GRGY

In 2022, GRGY and ChikoRoko created Kloud, a digital collectible toy bringing together two recognizable characters. The bright, friendly Roko meets Kloud’s dark palette, contrasting details, and distinctive character.

The collaboration translates GRGY’s visual language into the three-dimensional form of a digital toy.
